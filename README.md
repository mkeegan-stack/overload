# Overload

A personal gym app: builds a training plan around your equipment and goal, logs workouts, sets a target for every set, tracks records, body weight and measurements, and shows each exercise in 3D with the muscles it works.

**Use it:** open the site in Safari on iPhone (or Chrome on Android), then Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline once added.

Your data stays on your own phone. Use **Me → Export backup** to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

Credits: exercise list partly from [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) (public domain). 3D mannequin: X Bot from the [three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) examples (originally Mixamo). Rendering by three.js (MIT).
