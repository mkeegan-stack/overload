# Overload

A personal gym app: builds a training plan around your equipment and goal, logs workouts with a target for every set, warm-ups and a plate calculator, tracks records, body weight, measurements, progress photos and weekly muscle balance, suggests deload weeks, and shows each exercise in 3D with the muscles it works.

**Use it:** open the site in Safari on iPhone (or Chrome on Android), then Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline once added.

Your data, including photos, stays on your own phone. Use **Me → Export backup** to keep a copy or move to a new phone.
